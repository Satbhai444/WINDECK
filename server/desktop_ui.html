About WinDeck Server

WinDeck (Desktop) v2.3.7

WinDeck transforms your Android device into a highly customizable, zero-latency macro pad for Windows. Completely private and fully local.

Data Sharing & Privacy

WinDeck is completely local. No data is ever sent to any external server.

100% Local Network
All communication happens directly between your PC and phone over your local Wi-Fi network. WinDeck does not use the internet.
No Data Collection
WinDeck collects zero analytics, usage data, crash reports, or personal information. Your layout, device names, and settings stay only on your device.
No Third-Party Sharing
Your data is never shared with third parties. There are no ads, no tracking, and no telemetry services integrated in this app.
Files Transferred Stay Local
When you use DropZone to transfer files between your phone and PC, they are sent directly over your Wi-Fi — never through a cloud or external server.

Notifications

Windows toast notifications for device events.

Help Center — How to Use WinDeck

1 Start the Server

Open WinDeck on your PC. Click "Start Server" and enter a room name. A 6-digit Pairing Code will appear on screen.

2 Connect Your Phone

Open WinDeck on your Android phone. Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. The app will auto-discover your PC, or you can enter the Pairing Code manually.

3 Build Your Layout

Go to the Editor tab. Drag tiles from the panel (PC Apps, System Controls, Websites) onto the phone preview. Click "Save & Sync" — tiles appear on your phone instantly.

4 Transfer Files (DropZone)

To send a file from your Phone to PC: tap the DropZone tile on your phone, select a file — it lands in your PC's Downloads folder. To send from PC to Phone: click the "Send to Phone" box on the Dashboard, choose a file, and it opens on your phone automatically.

5 Rename Your Device

On the Dashboard, click the ✏️ pencil icon next to your connected device name to give it a custom label. It is saved for future sessions.

About WinDeck

WinDeck was born from a simple idea — why pay for a premium macro pad when your Android phone is right there? We wanted a fast, private, fully-local alternative that puts you in control.

Inspiration

WinDeck draws inspiration from PhoneDeck — a fantastic web-based tool that lets you use your phone as a stream deck. We love what they built. WinDeck takes a different approach: it runs fully on your local network, is open for customization, and targets Windows-native control rather than a browser-based experience.
