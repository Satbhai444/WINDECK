About Me

I'm Darshan Satbhai, the creator of WinDeck. I built this because I was tired of reaching for my mouse and breaking my flow state just to skip a song, change volume, or launch a quick app. I didn't want to spend $250 on a StreamDeck when I already had a perfectly capable supercomputer sitting on my desk—my Android phone.

WinDeck is my solution to that problem. A completely free, 100% local, ultra-fast Windows controller. I hope it helps you stay focused as much as it helped me.
