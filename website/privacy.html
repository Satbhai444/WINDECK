Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Nothing leaves your network

WinDeck is designed with absolute privacy in mind. The Android app and Windows Server communicate exclusively over your Local Area Network (LAN). No data, keystrokes, or commands are ever sent to external servers.

2. Zero Telemetry

We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or analytics. What you do with WinDeck is completely your business.

3. Encrypted Connection

All communication between your phone and PC is encrypted using AES-256-CBC. Even if someone intercepts your local Wi-Fi traffic, they cannot read your commands or keystrokes.
